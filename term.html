Terms of Service

Last updated: 15 October 2026

These are the terms under which Samridah Cleaning Services provides cleaning and supply services. They exist so both you and we know what to expect. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you book and we will explain it.

1. About us

Samridah Cleaning Services is registered with the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) and operates from Mutungo 1, Zone 1A, Nakawa Division, Kampala, Uganda.

Contact us at samridahservices@gmail.com or +256 746 200 441.

2. Booking a service

When you submit a booking request through our website, by phone, or by WhatsApp, you are requesting a quote. No contract is formed until we have confirmed the price and date with you in writing.

A booking is only confirmed once:

  1. We have confirmed the price for the job
  2. We have agreed the date and time
  3. You have replied to confirm you want to proceed

Until all three are done, either side can walk away without obligation.

3. Pricing and payment

Prices are quoted based on the information you give us. This includes the type of cleaning, the size and condition of the space, your location, and any special requirements.

If we arrive and find the actual situation is different from what was described, we may need to adjust the price. We will tell you before we start work, and you are free to decline and cancel.

Payment terms

  • Payment is due on completion of the work, unless agreed otherwise in writing
  • We accept cash, mobile money, and bank transfer
  • For jobs over UGX 500,000, we may ask for a deposit of 30 percent to secure the booking
  • For commercial contracts, invoicing and payment terms can be agreed separately
Late payment: If payment is not received within 14 days of the invoice date, we reserve the right to stop providing ongoing services until the balance is cleared.

4. Cancellations and changes

We understand that plans change. If you need to cancel or move your booking, tell us as soon as you can.

If you cancel

  • More than 24 hours before the booking: No charge. Any deposit is refunded or held for a future date, whichever you prefer.
  • Less than 24 hours before the booking: We may keep any deposit to cover the lost slot.
  • No access on the day: If our team arrives and cannot access the premises, the full price may be charged.

If we cancel

If we ever need to cancel or reschedule a booking, we will tell you as soon as we know and offer a new date or a full refund of any deposit paid. This is rare and only happens for genuine reasons such as illness or severe weather.

5. What we need from you

For us to do the job properly, we need:

  • Clear access to the property at the agreed time
  • Accurate information about the space before we quote
  • Water and electricity available on site
  • Any special instructions told to us before we start
  • Valuables, fragile items, and pets secured or moved

If any of these are missing, we may not be able to deliver the standard we normally do, and the job may need to be rescheduled.

6. What we are not responsible for

We treat every space with care, but there are certain things that fall outside our responsibility:

  • Pre-existing damage to surfaces, furniture, or fittings
  • Wear and tear that cleaning cannot reverse
  • Items left unsecured, unlocked, or in unsafe positions
  • Water or electricity failures during our visit
  • Results from cleaning products the client specifically requested us to use
  • Damage caused by pets or third parties during our visit

If you have fragile, antique, or high-value items in the space, tell us before we start so we can take extra care or avoid them.

7. If something goes wrong

If you are unhappy with the work, tell us within 24 hours of the job finishing. We will come back and fix the specific issue at no cost, or in the rare case that we cannot, we will discuss a fair resolution.

Our liability for any single job is limited to the price paid for that job. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses.

Please note: We cannot address concerns raised more than 24 hours after the job is completed, as we have no way to verify what has happened in the space since we left.

8. Our team

Our cleaners are trusted, background-checked, and trained in our standard cleaning procedures. We do not subcontract without telling you first.

Please treat our team with respect. If a client is abusive or threatening toward any member of our team, we reserve the right to end the job immediately and refuse future bookings.

9. Product supplies

When you order cleaning products or supplies from us (detergents, disinfectants, tissue, toilet supplies, and similar), the following applies:

  • Products are supplied as-is from our approved suppliers
  • Delivery times are agreed in advance and may vary by location
  • Payment for product supplies is due on delivery or on invoice, as agreed
  • Any issues with product quality must be raised within 7 days of delivery

10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. If we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. The terms that apply to your booking are the ones in place when you booked.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda. Any dispute that cannot be resolved between us will be handled by the courts of Uganda.
