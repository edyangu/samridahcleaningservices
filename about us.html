Real people, real work, since 2024.

Samridah Cleaning Services started with one simple idea: cleaning should be done properly, on time, and by people who actually care about the result. That has not changed.

Our story

Started in Kampala, built on trust.

We started Samridah because we saw too many homes and offices being cleaned to a standard that nobody would accept for their own space. Rushed jobs, missed corners, empty promises about coming back. We knew it could be done better.

So in 2024 we registered the business with URSB and started taking bookings across Kampala. From Mutungo to Nakawa, from family homes to offices and compounds, we've built a small but loyal client base by doing exactly what we say we will do. No shortcuts, no excuses.

Today we serve homes, businesses, and compounds across the city. Every job, big or small, gets the same attention. That is why our clients keep coming back, and why the ones who refer us to friends and neighbours do so without hesitation.

What we stand for

Three values we do not compromise on.

We show up on time

Time is the one thing we can all agree is precious. When we agree a date and a time, we arrive on that date at that time. No excuses, no last-minute changes. If something genuinely goes wrong, we tell you before you have to ask.

We clean to a real standard

We clean the way we would want our own homes cleaned. Corners, edges, under the furniture, behind the toilet, the parts that get skipped when someone is rushing. That is the standard every single job is held to.

We are accountable

Samridah is registered with URSB. We are a real, accountable business with a physical address, a working phone number, and a reputation we protect. Your home or office is in responsible hands, every single time.
