Questions

Where do you work?

We are based in Mutungo, Nakawa Division, and we serve homes and businesses across Kampala.

Yes. We are available every day of the week, including Sundays.

We reply to every request within a day. For something urgent, call or WhatsApp us on 0746 200 441.
