Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Samridah Cleaning Services ("we", "us", "our") is a cleaning company registered in Uganda with the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Who we are

Samridah Cleaning Services

We are the data controller for any personal information you provide to us through this website or in the course of doing business with us.

2. What data we collect

When you submit a booking request through our website, we collect:

  • Your name — so we know who to contact
  • Your email address — so we can reply to your request
  • Your phone number — so we can reach you quickly
  • The service you requested — so we know what to quote for
  • Any details you include — such as your location, property size, or preferred date

When you leave feedback on our website, we collect:

  • Your name — shown publicly with your comment
  • Your comment text — shown publicly on the website
  • The date and time you submitted it

We do not collect payment information through this website. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

3. Why we collect it

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your booking request — this is the main reason we ask for your details
  • To provide the cleaning service you've requested
  • To contact you about your booking — confirming dates, prices, or changes
  • To display your feedback publicly — only if you choose to submit it
  • To keep basic business records — as required for our URSB registration

We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers. We never use it for marketing you didn't ask for.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run our website and receive your messages:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — stores your booking request securely so we can view it in our admin dashboard. Firebase is provided by Google LLC. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Formspree — forwards your booking request to our email inbox so we're notified instantly. See Formspree Privacy.
  • Vercel — hosts this website. See Vercel Privacy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this page.

We do not share your data with any other third parties. If we ever need to, we will ask your permission first.

5. How long we keep it

Booking requests: we keep them for up to 12 months after your job is completed, then delete them.

Feedback you post publicly: kept until you ask us to remove it, or until we retire the website.

Business records: kept as long as required by Ugandan tax and business regulations.

6. Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • See what we hold about you — ask us and we'll send it
  • Correct anything wrong — tell us and we'll fix it
  • Delete your data — ask us and we'll remove it, unless we're legally required to keep it
  • Withdraw consent — for anything you previously agreed to
  • Object to processing — tell us and we'll stop, unless we have a legal reason to continue

To exercise any of these, just email us at samridahservices@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. How we protect your data

Your booking data is stored securely in Google Firebase with access restricted to our admin panel. Only logged-in staff (using a verified email and password) can view your booking details. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections at all times.

Security note: No online system is 100% secure. If you're ever concerned about a booking you've submitted, contact us and we'll review and delete if needed.

8. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use tracking cookies. We don't run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

We do use browser local storage for one purpose only: to remember that our admin has signed in, so they don't have to log in on every page refresh. This data stays on the admin's own device and is never sent to us.

Because we don't use tracking cookies, you won't see a cookie consent banner — there's nothing to consent to.

9. Children

Our services are aimed at adults booking for their homes or businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has submitted data through this site, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll make it clear on the website. Continuing to use our site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, contact us first at samridahservices@gmail.com so we can try to fix it. If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) of Uganda:

  • Website: pdpo.go.ug
  • Email: info@pdpo.go.ug
